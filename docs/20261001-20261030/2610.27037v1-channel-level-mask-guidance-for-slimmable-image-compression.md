## 摘要
学习式图像压缩（LIC）已取得有前景的率失真性能，但大多数方法缺乏面向计算与存储约束各异的多样化平台的可扩展性。现有的可伸缩 LIC 方法通过连续通道激活实现灵活复杂度，但存在两个局限：强制连续通道选择会降低窄模型在低码率下的性能，且缺乏稳定的可变码率控制限制了其对变化带宽的适应性。我们提出一种通道级掩码引导的可伸缩图像压缩方法。带有稀疏促进正则化的可学习通道级掩码可实现非连续的 top-w 通道选择，使窄模型能够使用最具信息量的通道。训练后，通过 Mask-to-Slimmable（M2S）转换进行掩码合并与通道重排序，将模型转换为标准可伸缩网络，从而无需改变架构即可实现高效推理。对于可变码率控制，我们将 lambda 与宽度条件调制同量化步长缩放相结合，并采用由先冻结后微调策略稳定的渐进训练流程。在 Kodak 数据集上，基于 E2E、Hyperprior 和 Checkerboard 主干网络的实验表明，在不同宽度和码率下均具有更优的率失真性能，且窄模型在低码率下获得显著增益。当 w=48 时，在 E2E、Hyperprior 和 CKBD 上，相较于同宽度 Slimmable 基线，分别最高实现 19.22%、24.11% 和 22.76% 的 BD-rate 节省。单个 VBR 模型可替代 40 个独立模型，将存储占用降低 14.8 至 15.9 倍。代码：https://github.com/sjtu-tcloud/MGSIC。

## Abstract
Learned image compression (LIC) has achieved promising rate-distortion performance, but most methods lack scalability for diverse platforms with varying computational and storage constraints. Existing slimmable LIC methods achieve flexible complexity via consecutive-channel activation but suffer from two limitations: forced consecutive-channel selection degrades narrow-model performance at low bitrates, and the lack of stable variable-rate control limits adaptability to varying bandwidth. We propose a channel-level mask-guided slimmable image compression method. A learnable channel-wise mask with sparsity-promoting regularization enables non-contiguous top-w channel selection, letting narrow models use the most informative channels. After training, mask merging and channel reordering convert the model into a standard slimmable network via Mask-to-Slimmable (M2S) conversion, enabling efficient inference without architectural changes. For variable-rate control, we combine lambda- and width-conditioned modulation with quantization-step scaling and a progressive training pipeline stabilized by a freeze-then-fine-tune strategy. Experiments on Kodak across E2E, Hyperprior, and Checkerboard backbones show superior rate-distortion performance across widths and bitrates, with large gains for narrow models at low bitrates. At w=48, it achieves up to 19.22%, 24.11%, and 22.76% BD-rate savings over the same-width Slimmable baseline on E2E, Hyperprior, and CKBD, respectively. A single VBR model replaces 40 independent models, reducing storage by 14.8x to 15.9x. Code: https://github.com/sjtu-tcloud/MGSIC.
