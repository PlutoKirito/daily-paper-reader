## 摘要
在严格的码率约束下，现有视频编解码器难以平衡源保真度与感知真实感。面向失真的编解码器常常过度平滑细节，而生成式编解码器则可能引入内容和结构偏差，并依赖于编解码器特定的设计。这引出一个问题：现有编解码器能否通过简单、可复用的适配实现更好的失真—感知权衡？我们的洞见是，原生编解码器重建提供了一种共享接口，通过该接口，生成式细化既能锚定于源内容，又能与编解码器特定表示解耦。因此，我们提出 Gen2-VC，一种生成式视频压缩框架，它利用预训练视频先验增强学习式与传统编解码器的输出，同时不改变其比特流和参考更新过程。在编解码器、VAE 和生成主干网络冻结的情况下，轻量级 LoRA 适配器通过单帧空间适配和随后的多帧时间适配，借助视频先验对编解码器重建进行细化。使用 Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B，Gen2-VC-DCVC-UF 在 LPIPS/DISTS 上优于此前领先的编解码器，并且据我们所知，是首个在低码率下同时在 PSNR 和 MS-SSIM 上超越 VTM-23.0 的生成式视频编解码器，该结果基于在六个数据集上平均的 BD-rate。与 VTM-23.0 相比，在匹配 LPIPS 和 DISTS 时，它分别平均降低码率 86.65% 和 94.24%。仅在 DCVC-UF 上训练的适配器无需重新训练即可提升 DCVC-RT、ECM、VTM 和 HM 的感知质量。

## Abstract
Under stringent bitrate constraints, existing video codecs struggle to balance source fidelity and perceptual realism. Distortion-oriented codecs often oversmooth details, while generative codecs risk introducing content and structural deviations and rely on codec-specific designs. This motivates a question: Can existing codecs achieve a better distortion--perception trade-off through simple, reusable adaptation? Our insight is that native codec reconstructions provide a shared interface through which generative refinement is anchored to source content while remaining decoupled from codec-specific representations. We therefore propose Gen2-VC, a generative video compression framework that enhances the outputs of learned and conventional codecs with a pretrained video prior, leaving their bitstreams and reference update processes unchanged. With the codec, VAE, and generative backbone frozen, lightweight LoRA adapters refine codec reconstruction through single-frame spatial adaptation followed by multi-frame temporal adaptation with the video prior. Using Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, Gen2-VC-DCVC-UF outperforms previous leading codecs in LPIPS/DISTS and, to our knowledge, is the first generative video codec to surpass VTM-23.0 in both PSNR and MS-SSIM at low bitrates, based on BD-rates averaged over six datasets. Compared to VTM-23.0, it reduces bitrate by an average of 86.65% and 94.24% at matched LPIPS and DISTS, respectively. Adapters trained only on DCVC-UF improve perceptual quality on DCVC-RT, ECM, VTM, and HM without retraining.
