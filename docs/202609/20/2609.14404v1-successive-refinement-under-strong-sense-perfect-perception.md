## 摘要
我们重新审视一个名为逐次细化的多终端有损信源编码问题，并在存在无限公共随机性的情况下，推导强意义完美感知约束下的率-失真-感知区域。具体而言，在逐次细化中，目标是对信源序列进行压缩，并允许两个不同的解码器以不同的失真水平恢复该信源序列。通过施加强意义完美感知约束，我们的结果通过分析感知质量的影响改进了先前的结果。我们的可达性证明受输出约束有损信源编码启发，而我们的逆定理证明改编了标准逐次细化问题的证明步骤。此外，我们给出了伯努利信源的数值示例来说明我们的结果，并表明在汉明失真下，即使存在强意义完美感知约束，伯努利信源仍是可逐次细化的。

## Abstract
We revisit a multiterminal lossy source coding problem named successive refinement and derive the rate-distortion-perception region under the strong-sense perfect perception constraint in the presence of unlimited common randomness. Specifically, in successive refinement, one aims to compress a source sequence and allows two distinct decoders to recover the source sequence at different distortion levels. By imposing the strong-sense perfect perception constraint, our results refine the previous result by analyzing the impact of the perceptual quality. Our achievability proof is inspired by output constrained lossy source coding and our converse proof adapts the proof steps of the standard successive refinement problem. Furthermore, we provide a numerical example of the Bernoulli source to illustrate our result and show that the Bernoulli source under Hamming distortion is successively refinable even with the strong-sense perfect perception constraint.
