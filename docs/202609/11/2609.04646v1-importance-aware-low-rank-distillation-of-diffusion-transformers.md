## 摘要
扩散Transformer（DiT）已成为高质量文本到图像生成的主导架构，但其规模给高效部署带来了挑战。尽管截断奇异值分解（SVD）是一种有原则的参数压缩工具，但来自大语言模型（LLM）的证据表明，朴素的低秩近似可能导致灾难性失败。相反，我们发现，在DiT中，即使进行大幅全局压缩，截断SVD也只会带来平滑的性能退化，冗余分布在整个网络的投影矩阵中，而非集中在少数Transformer块中。基于这些洞见，我们提出SVDtrunc，一种两步式的块级压缩方案：首先在全局参数预算下为各块分配秩，并通过截断SVD压缩最不重要的块；然后使用模块化知识蒸馏和整流流目标对所有块进行微调。我们将SVDtrunc应用于FLUX.dev，覆盖原始参数量40%–90%的压缩水平。在GenEval、HPSv2和DPG三个基准上，我们优于所有竞争方法。值得注意的是，与先前工作不同，我们在原始参数预算的68%时仍保持接近完整性能，即使在57%时也仍具竞争力。此外，我们表明SVDtrunc可与步数蒸馏互补，并且即使不进行微调也能取得强劲结果，使其成为大规模生成模型在扩散步数减少之外效率提升的实际延续。   项目主页：https://vislearn.github.io/SVDtrunc/

## Abstract
Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) have emerged as a dominant architecture for high-quality text-to-image generation, yet their scale poses challenges for efficient deployment. While truncated singular value decomposition (SVD) is a principled tool for parameter reduction, evidence from large language models (LLMs) suggests that naive low-rank approximation can cause catastrophic failure. In contrast, we find that truncated SVD in DiTs produces smooth degradation even under substantial global compression, with redundancy distributed across projection matrices throughout the whole network rather than concentrated in a few transformer blocks. Building on these insights, we introduce SVDtrunc, a two-step block-level compression scheme, first allocating ranks across blocks and compressing the least important ones via truncated SVD under a global parameter budget, and then fine-tuning all blocks with modular knowledge distillation and a rectified-flow objective. We apply SVDtrunc to FLUX.dev across compression levels ranging from 40-90% of the original parameter count. Across three benchmarks, GenEval, HPSv2, and DPG, we outperform all competing approaches. Notably, and in contrast to prior work, we retain near-full performance at 68% and remain competitive even at 57% of the original parameter budget. Furthermore, we show that SVDtrunc complements step distillation and achieves strong results even without fine-tuning, positioning it as a practical continuation of efficiency improvements beyond diffusion step reduction for large-scale generative models.
